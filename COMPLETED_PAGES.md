## Explanation of Makeup Classes

**Purpose:** Makeup Classes module handles rescheduling for students who missed their regular classes.

**Workflow:**
1. **Student Absent** - Marked absent in Daily Attendance
2. **Auto-Create Record** - System creates a "Pending" makeup class entry
3. **Admin Schedules** - Admin assigns new date, time, teacher, room
4. **Student Notified** - Automatic notification sent to student
5. **Completion** - After attending makeup session, marked as "Completed"
6. **Progress Updated** - Student's attendance record updated

**Why It's Needed:**
- Ensures students don't fall behind if they miss classes
- Maintains attendance requirements for certification
- Provides flexibility for emergencies, medical leave
- Tracks makeup session completion
- Helps academy comply with course completion standards
